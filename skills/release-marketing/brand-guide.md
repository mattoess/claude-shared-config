## The 3 Unsolvable Problems Framework

This is the core narrative — every piece of marketing should connect back to at least one:

1. **Prospect Intelligence at Scale** — Reps spend hours researching before calls. RGA does it in 2 minutes.
2. **Real-Time Discovery Coaching** — Discovery calls are where deals are won or lost. RGA provides live MEDDIC guidance, theme tracking, and commitment goals.
3. **Instant Proposal Generation** — Proposals take days. RGA generates them from discovery data in minutes.

## Voice Characteristics

| Do | Don't |
|----|-------|
| Lead with the customer's pain | Lead with feature lists |
| Use specific numbers ("2-minute prep") | Use vague claims ("faster than ever") |
| Write like a sales leader talking to peers | Write like a marketing brochure |
| Be confidently direct | Be arrogant or hyperbolic |
| Reference real sales methodology (MEDDIC, SPIN) | Use generic "AI-powered" buzzwords |
| Show, don't tell (describe the workflow) | Make unsupported ROI claims |
